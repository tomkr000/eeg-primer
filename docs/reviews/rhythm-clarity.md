## Scientific corrections

- The old sensorimotor exception to artifact rejection was removed. Facial muscle activity can contaminate even central recordings: [Goncharova et al. 2003](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12948787/).
- Gamma interference is described as contamination, not a universal claim that scalp gamma is muscle. The claim registry now limits the blockade result to its recording conditions: [Whitham et al. 2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17574912/). Neural gamma can be recovered after artifact handling: [Hipp and Siegel 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23847508/).
- The readiness-potential tangent no longer implies a reliable individual prediction of intention. Averaged buildup and individual intention are different: [Schurger et al. 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3479453/).
- The travelling-wave claim no longer treats 10–12 cm inflated electrode coherence as a hard spatial-resolution cutoff. Its negative conclusion is scoped to the cited studies: [Srinivasan et al. 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9644890/), [Takahashi et al. 2011](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2011.00040/full), [Alamia and VanRullen 2019](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3000487).
- Removed the assertion that the same band-power scatter limit applies unchanged to every slow potential and other control feature.

## Broad regions, local patches and signed contributions

Added a compact figure immediately above the rhythm map. Dashed regions contain numbered local patches drawn with section 4's cortical cloud and patch glows. The adjacent traces show patches 1 and 2 contributing voltage at electrode E, followed by their sum. Reinforce, Timing drifts and Cancel keep the same vertical scale. Region boundaries, patch counts and equal signal strengths are explicitly illustrative; the waveforms are not calculated from the displayed anatomical lead field. Other patches and noise are excluded from this pair's sum.

An independent source checker reviewed partial correlations in [Łęski et al. 2013](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1003137), state-dependent synchrony in the primary abstract of [Poulet and Petersen 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18633351/), and orientation-dependent cancellation in [Irimia et al. 2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3254784/). The claim registry distinguishes a computational extracellular-field model and nearby mouse-neuron recordings from measurements of human EEG patches. It does not infer independence from weak correlation or distance.

The check also exposed an incorrect inference in section 2: EEG sensitivity to individual source orientations does not mean that cortical folding hides little when several sources are active. The text now explains that folding can make their signed contributions cancel. Section 4's caption calls patches local groups of cortical cells, consistent with its adjustable fraction in step, rather than implying perfect internal synchrony.

The caption now explains only the visible equal-and-opposite contributions. A separate statement explains that current direction can also cause cancellation with synchronized currents. The same review prompted stronger region boundaries, larger explanatory notes, a bold pair-only caveat, and simpler between-patch wording. On a second reading the reviewer found no blocking confusion. This remains a language-model comprehension check, not a human user study.
