# Verification log

Every claim on the site was fact-checked by an auditor that had not seen the site, the research notes, or the
other auditors' work. Each auditor received a batch of claims with their citations and was instructed to find
errors: open the cited source, locate the statement, and report SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / NOT_SUPPORTED / NO_ACCESS
with the evidence location and any discrepancy. Where a discrepancy was found, the claim text in
`src/data/claims.ts` was corrected and the correction is noted below; the status badge on the site reflects the
corrected statement.

Auditor verdicts on the original statements (143 claims with sources): SUPPORTED 83, PARTIAL 56, NO_ACCESS 4.
Claims checked by hand after an auditor could not open the source say so in their rows. Claims added in November
2026 (sections 7, 9 and 11 and the conclusion) were checked directly against the full texts of their sources, without a separate
audit, and their rows say "checked by hand". Resolved statuses (189 claims): verified 139, derived 18, partially 10,
assumption 21, unverified 1.

An outside citation audit in November 2026 checked every bibliography entry and raised content findings; the
corrections it prompted are noted in the affected rows ("citation audit").

Section 12 (named brain states, added in November 2026) was audited in six batches (22 to 27, `docs/audit/`) by
auditors who had not seen the site; its 28 claims appear in the table with their batch numbers, and every discrepancy
they raised was folded into the claim text.

## Known limitations recorded against claims
- Electrode impedances (Chi et al. 2010, Table I) were measured on the forearm with no skin-preparation details and no stated electrode area; the site uses them as per-cm² reference values for scalp electrodes.
- The scalp-thickness value rests on a two-patient case report stating a normal range; no normative study was located.
- The dura-thickness value is from the petrous apex (skull base), not the convexity.
- The relative permittivity of a hair/air gap is a modelling assumption (air, εr = 1).
- The dimensionality count in section 9 (claim svd-counts) is a model-derived upper bound (flat source spectrum; "above the noise floor" criterion).
- The shoulder patch's 85% response to shoulder imagery and its lack of crosstalk are assumptions (claim shoulder-area).
- Section 11's finger regions are as broad as the hand area, chosen so that thumb against little finger lands near the measured 81% (claim finger-model); real finger imagery also shares much of its change across the hand area, which the model leaves out.
- The spacing of neighboring fingers assumes the five fingers are evenly spaced (claim finger-neighbors).
- The sizes of the chewing, nodding and head-rolling artifacts in section 7 are read from figure axes of one consumer headset recording (claim head-movement-artifacts).
- Section 12's state factors are model inputs: effect sizes become power factors by a chosen rule (claim g-to-ratio), the sleep, anesthesia, memory and meditation sizes are chosen within the sources' directions, and the arousal and valence sizes are fitted to measured single-trial accuracies (claim state-table); the resting amplitudes rest on glossary bounds (claim rest-amplitudes) and the per-band background on a chosen 15 µV rms total (claim band-background); the between-person spread is read from a figure (claim between-person).
